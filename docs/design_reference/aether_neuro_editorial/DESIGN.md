## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated, high-fashion clinical aesthetic tailored for neuro-technology, diagnostics, and human cognitive performance platforms. The visual identity establishes a bridge between advanced biometrics and high-end editorial curation. It rejects the cold, hyper-synthetic neon tropes of typical medical tech, leaning instead into tactile, museum-grade calm and precision.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Sculptural & Scientific:** Rigorous clinical data presented with architectural grace, balancing micro-level diagnostic clarity with calming macro compositions.
- **Understated Opulence:** High-fashion tactility expressed through warm alabaster fields, cashmere neutrals, and structural bronze-slate accents.
- **Introspective Equilibrium:** The visual environment reduces cognitive friction, inviting deliberate review and contemplative analysis.

### Design Movement
A fusion of **Warm Editorial Minimalism** and **Tactile Neomorphism/Glassmorphism**. Structural framing relies on airy spatial ratios, delicate hairline borders, pill-shaped tags, and deep espresso-bronze structural accents, paired with ultra-refined frosted glass planes.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from organic stone, warm alabaster, and patinated bronze. Surfaces prioritize warmth and high legibility without pure optical black or glaring white.

### Palette Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#4A4641`):** Deep bronze-slate. Grounding anchor for primary action surfaces, structural dividers, and dominant iconography.
- **Secondary (`#B87355`):** Subtle terracotta-clay. Used intentionally for state transitions, neural telemetry highlights, and focal status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C8BEAF`):** Cashmere nude. Applied across secondary badges, tactile surface borders, and subtle structural infills.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` / `#F5F2EB`):** Pure warm ivory and alabaster backgrounds. Creates an expansive, glare-free optical canvas.
- **Typography (`#1A1918` / `#2E2B28`):** Deep espresso-charcoal. Provides editorial contrast against light backgrounds while avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Hairline Dividing Lines (`#E5DFD5`):** Delicate boundary tones providing soft structural containment.

## Typography

Typography balances the geometric modernism of **Outfit** for headlines and display tiers with the ergonomic, humanist clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for running body text and telemetry labels.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Rendered in `Outfit` with restrained font weights (400 to 500) and tight letter spacing to project quiet authority and editorial polish.
- **Body & Clinical Readings:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with relaxed leading to maximize scanning comfort across dense diagnostic reports.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Set in uppercase, tracked letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to mimic medical instrument readouts while preserving an elevated, architectural rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model favors expansive negative space, establishing an uncluttered rhythm that reduces diagnostic fatigue.

### Layout Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` section margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers capped at an optimal reading width of `1320px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Two-column clinical readouts collapse into unified modular stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Dense metric panels convert to horizontal scrollable tracks or singular full-width cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal card and section padding follows an architectural scale. Large gaps (`space-xl` / `2.5rem`) are used to isolate discrete neurological data modules, ensuring metrics never compete visually.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through multi-tiered tonal layering, translucent glassmorphism, and diffused ambient warmth. Opaque drop shadows and high-contrast cutouts are avoided.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#FBF9F5` (warm ivory) with micro-textures or faint gradient fades into `#F5F2EB`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** Semi-opaque frosted glass (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.75)`) backed by a `16px` backdrop-blur filter and bounded by a `1px` stroke of `#E5DFD5`.
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Floating Panels):** `#FFFFFF` at 90% opacity, paired with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(74, 70, 65, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 70, 65, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Contextual Flyouts):** Suspended above a tinted backdrop overlay (`rgba(46, 43, 40, 0.25)` with `8px` blur), framed in `#E5DFD5`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** roundedness strategy. Rounded contours soften the clinical precision of diagnostic instrumentation, conveying organic human warmth.

- **Base Corner Radius:** `1rem` for standard components, inner utility containers, and input fields.
- **Pill Geometry:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) styling reserved for interactive buttons, clinical status badges, and filter pills.
- **Containers & Master Cards:** `rounded-lg` (`2rem`) and `rounded-xl` (`3rem`) for high-level module bounding boxes.
- **Borders:** Consistent `1px` continuous hairlines in `#E5DFD5`, keeping visual frames light and delicate.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), `#4A4641` fill with `#FBF9F5` text. On hover, transitions gently to `#2E2B28`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Full pill radius, transparent background with a `1px` border of `#C8BEAF`, text in `#4A4641`. On hover, subtle ivory fill (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Terracotta Accent:** Full pill radius, `#B87355` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, reserved for critical primary confirmations or active telemetry states.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Compact pill height with `0.5rem` vertical and `1rem` horizontal padding.
- Text set in `label-sm` uppercase.
- Neutral active: `#E8E2D8` background with `#2E2B28` text.
- Signal status: Subtle terracotta wash (`rgba(184, 115, 85, 0.12)`) with `#B87355` text and a `6px` solid status dot.

### Cards & Modular Panels
- Frosted ivory surface (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.8)`) with `2rem` rounded corners and a delicate `1px` boundary in `#E5DFD5`.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- Headers pair an `Outfit` headline with tracked `label-md` diagnostic tags.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height of `3rem`, pill or `1rem` rounded corners.
- Background in `#F5F2EB`, border in `#E5DFD5`, focused border transitioning to `#4A4641` with a `2px` faint ring (`rgba(74, 70, 65, 0.08)`).
- Placeholder text in `#C8BEAF`, typed text in `#1A1918`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- Checkboxes feature soft `0.375rem` rounded corners with `#E5DFD5` borders. Checked states fill with `#4A4641`.
- Switch toggles use a full pill container (`1.5rem` height by `2.75rem` width) in `#E8E2D8`, sliding an ivory circle knob smoothly into `#4A4641` on activation.

### Telemetry & Neuro-Data Displays
- Numeric readings set in `Outfit` medium with baseline-aligned `Plus Jakarta Sans` units.
- Waveforms and biometric sparklines rendered in clean `1.5px` strokes of `#B87355` or `#4A4641` against transparent grid meshes.
